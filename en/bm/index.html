BedrockMap

Free, open-source, high-performance cross-platform Minecraft Bedrock world editor with NetEase Edition support

BedrockMap uses C++20 to read and write LevelDB directly, with fast chunk loading and map rendering. Open multiple worlds and switch between independent workspaces without losing your place.

Capabilities

Built for large worlds and parallel work

Multiple worlds / workspaces

Open several worlds at once and switch between independent map, NBT and structure workspaces without closing your current task.

World browsing

Open international and NetEase Edition worlds and explore every chunk of the Overworld, Nether, The End and custom dimensions.

Terrain visualization

Biome maps and terrain overlays, with configurable rendering filters.

Chunk editor

Select, inspect and delete chunks, and visualize the entities, block entities and pending ticks inside them.

Chunk copy & paste

Copy a selected region into another world — even across saves — with interactive placement.

NBT editor

View and modify NBT data, including level.dat, player inventories, villages, map items and more.

mcstructure support

Browse and edit .mcstructure files with a 3D voxel preview, export selections as mcstructure or GLB, or import structures into a world.

Map and chunk controls
